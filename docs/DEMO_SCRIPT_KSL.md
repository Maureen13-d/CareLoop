# Demo script: KSL path

Synthetic data only. Do not tell a viewer that the motion is Kenyan Sign Language.

## Patient card

1. Open the card. Choose Kenyan Sign Language before sending the code.
2. Continue with a demo ID. The reply is captions. The banner reads `PLACEHOLDER MOTION: NOT KSL`.
3. Play the test motion. It is a wave. Say that out loud if you are narrating.
4. Point at the line "Recognition not enabled in this build." Answers are taps. The camera is off.
5. Tap "I feel worse". The core still asks a person to follow up. It does not say the person is fine.

English remains the default. The spoken guide is unchanged when English is selected.

## Staff

1. Sign in as a clinician or admin.
2. Open `/ksl`. The list is empty until someone uploads a clip. That is the honest state of this build.
3. There is no approved clip and no `ksl-ready` tag. Do not add one to suggest otherwise.

## Do not show

- A phone call as the main action for a KSL follow-up.
- Any raw signer video. None is in the repository.
- A frame-rate number. This build was not measured on a phone.
