# Data declaration

Every person, contact, screening, message, and metric in this repository is **synthetic**.

- No hospital, public-health programme, or patient provided data.
- No external dataset was imported.
- Demo names (Amina, David, Grace, and the maternal stories) are fictional.
- Follow-up journeys are identified by random IDs. The cohort of 200 is generated by `build_cohort` with a fixed seed.
- Phone numbers in the seed and the cohort are made-up strings.

Organisers of the Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge supplied no data. Nothing here should be quoted as a Kenyan coverage rate or as evidence that follow-up works.
