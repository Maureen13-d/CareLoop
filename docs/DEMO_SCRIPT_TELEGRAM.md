# Telegram demo (about 3 minutes)

Synthetic people only. The bot is not a doctor, and a chat with a bot is not end-to-end encrypted.

1. Sign in as the clinician and open a Telegram enrolment. Print the slip. The QR opens the bot with a one-time link.
2. In the bot: English, OK, the ID on the card, then the simulated code. The menu appears.
3. Day 2 check-in: Not yet, Transport, Ask a person to help. The bot says a person will help. The worklist shows the barrier.
4. Day 4: Worse. The bot drops the friendly tone and says to go to the nearest clinic now. An urgent task is open. Example clinic buttons are labelled as demo places.
5. The navigator sees the person on the chase list. The clinic, not the bot, confirms arrival. The next status message celebrates. The facility board moves to arrived.

The same lines are on the staff page at `/telegram`. That page does not send anything to Telegram.
